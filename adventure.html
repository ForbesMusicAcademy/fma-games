Each move on its own. Slow it down, drag the scrubber, or turn on onion skin to see the whole arc.

How a quest works here

  • Tap 1: the character runs the route, with a different move for every obstacle.
  • Tap 2: the quest card opens and launches the real game.
  • Clearing it gives the gear for that spot, and you wear it straight away.

The new character rig

  • Side and back views are a jointed puppet built from the student's own character parts, so it's the same character from every side.
  • Moves are a handful of key poses, so adding a new one is a short list, not new drawing.
  • On arrival the character turns to camera, using the same front art the other games use.
