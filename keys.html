Catch the notes on the keys

Notes fall down onto the keyboard. Play each one as it reaches the keys. Start with Learn (the notes wait for you), then try Play along with the music.

Play on a keyboard plugged into this device, a real piano (the microphone listens), the keys on the screen, or a computer keyboard (A S D F… are the white keys).

Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Hearing: –
Timing delay (raise it if notes land late)
