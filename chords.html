Play the chord you see

Your device listens through its microphone and lights up each note it hears. Hit every note of the chord to score.

Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. The sound is analysed on this device and thrown away.

Tip: find a quiet spot, tune your guitar first, and don't play music or videos through the speaker while you practise.
