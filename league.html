Every group class is a band. Help your band by playing the practice games at home: +1 a round (up to 5 a day), +3 for each day you practise, +3 for beating your own best, and a bonus when the whole band has practised. Your band also earns points together in class (beating the boss, team strikes, everyone in tune). Bands are ranked on points per member, so small bands can win too. Resets every Monday, with a new scene for the bands to act out each week.
