Lock in with the band

Ten levels of bass grooves. In each one the band plays a groove, then it's your turn to play it back. Every groove has three stars: Copy it, Lock it in (keep it going on your own) and By ear (no tab).

Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Your device listens through its microphone and throws the sound away as it goes.

Tips for playing with a phone or laptop

Put the device close to your amp (or close to your bass if it's not plugged in), and turn the device's volume up so you can hear the hi-hats on your turn.

Mute each note when the next one starts, especially in the levels with gaps. Clean starts and stops are what the game listens for, and they're what makes a groove sound tight.

Headphones are fine: then the game can't hear itself at all.
